# Third-party data, code and citations

## MIT KEMAR

Gardner, W. G. and Martin, K. D., “HRTF measurements of a KEMAR,” Journal of the Acoustical Society of America 97, 3907–3908 (1995); measurements recorded 1994. Source and terms: https://sound.media.mit.edu/resources/KEMAR.html . Copyright 1994 MIT Media Laboratory; data provided free with no restrictions on use provided the authors are cited.

Our derived JSON retains compact horizontal responses (0° to 180° at 5° steps), original 44.1 kHz/128 samples, signed-16-bit scale divided by 32768. Negative angles swap ears. No individualized measurement or BTE microphone data is implied.

## LibriSpeech

Panayotov, V., Chen, G., Povey, D. and Khudanpur, S., “LibriSpeech: an ASR corpus based on public domain audio books,” ICASSP 2015. https://www.openslr.org/12 . License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ .

Original test-clean utterances 6930-75918-0000 and 6930-75918-0007 are included as FLAC and converted WAV. Both are the same speaker. The browser rescales and loops them. Derived MSBG/NAL-R WAV files are marked by their filenames and generated by research/reference_study.py; they are not original corpus recordings. No endorsement by the corpus authors is implied.

## Clarity

https://github.com/claritychallenge/clarity ; installed evaluator version pyclarity 0.9.0. Its upstream licensing and notices apply to that dependency; no source files are vendored here. NAL-R, HASPI v2 and MSBG are called from the dependency. Citation information is in the white paper. Our browser filter bank is an independent approximation, not a port of the complete MSBG model.

## Literature

Bibliographic references and original URLs are embedded in dist/white-paper.tex and dist/survey-data.json. Published model performance is not presented as a local measurement. Neural weights and paper PDFs are not redistributed.
